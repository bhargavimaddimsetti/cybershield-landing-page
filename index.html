Why Cybersecurity Matters

Every day, people use the internet for communication, education, banking and entertainment. Good cybersecurity habits help keep personal information and digital accounts protected.

3 Simple Safety Tips

🔐 Use Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords for important accounts and avoid using easily guessed information.

🎣 Avoid Phishing

Check links and messages carefully before sharing passwords, payment information or personal details.

🛡️ Enable 2FA

Two-factor authentication adds another layer of protection when someone tries to access your account.

Think Before You Click

A few careful habits can make your digital life safer.
